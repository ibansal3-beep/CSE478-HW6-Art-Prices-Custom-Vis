02 / CURATOR'S NOTE

What makes a work
valuable?

There is no single answer in these records. Works created in the same year or assigned to the same movement can carry very different prices. This exhibit invites you to notice the variation rather than assume that age or style determines worth.

03 / DESIGN & METHOD

How this exhibit was made

Marks: Each artwork is an original five-petal radial glyph with a central disc. Channels: Petal radius encodes price (square-root scale); color encodes movement; horizontal position encodes exact creation year; vertical band encodes art period. Interaction: Hover or focus for artwork metadata; filter by period using the buttons above.

Custom visualization category — combinatorial: This design combines a time-positioned, period-banded visual field with bespoke radial flower glyphs that encode individual records. The flower geometry is generated with custom SVG paths in D3, rather than reproducing a stock D3 block or merely linking ordinary charts.

Data: Kaggle, “Art Price Dataset” (artDataset.csv). This exhibit uses 213 selected records from the source, requiring a valid numeric price, an exact four-digit creation year, and a known period and movement. Records were sampled across art periods and price quintiles to show a broad range; it is not a statistically representative estimate of the full market. Price strings were converted from USD notation into numbers. Original visualization code; no external D3 example or block was adapted. D3.js v7 is used for scales, SVG construction, and interaction.

Prices are recorded dataset values, not verified auction results or inflation-adjusted estimates. Artwork illustrations are abstract data symbols, not reproductions of the original art.
